  classification; no synthetic fixture can use HUMAN_REVIEW evidence.
- Exact concept/source-name set or recipe ID/source position/source row SHA;
  preserve ingredient restriction identity and original quantity text.
- Decision, unresolved reason or competing interpretations; original source
  versus shopping/process/consumed quantities and per-channel scaling.

  reference, subject SHA and exact normalized decision SHA.
- Source URL/role/content SHA/observedAt and the precise claim/line mapping;
  license/access/retention constraints and any changed or unreachable content.

  no universal spoon/cup/count/package or absorption assumption.

  nutrient evidence; blocked/null remains absent from numeric runtime macros.
