# Allergen and food safety policy

Unknown is preserved as unknown. Absence of an allergen tag is not a
no-allergen claim.

Candidate checks must trace allergen evidence through canonical ingredient
identity and substitutions. The review vocabulary includes fish, crustacean,
mollusk, egg, milk, soy, wheat/gluten, peanut, tree nut, and sesame, but does
not assert that production taxonomy supports every group today.

Cooking safety advice requires a named authoritative source. Recipe blogs and
AI text can suggest research questions but cannot create certified temperature,
storage, or cross-contamination guidance.
