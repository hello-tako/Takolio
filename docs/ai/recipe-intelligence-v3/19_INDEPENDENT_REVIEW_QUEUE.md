# Independent review queue

All 500 rows are review-required because every row contains at least one
ingredient identity issue under the V3 policy. The queue is generated by
`RECIPE_V3_DATA_QUALITY.csv`; its priority is triage only.

Review lanes:

1. source content and culinary claims;
2. ingredient identity and quantity transformations;
3. nutrition evidence and edible portions;
4. planner/shopping/cooking snapshot compatibility;
5. final release and rollback.

AI output, automated validation, and self-review cannot mark a row
`HUMAN_APPROVED`. Every approved row needs a reviewer identity, evidence
references, decision timestamp, candidate fingerprint, and unresolved-risk
disposition.
